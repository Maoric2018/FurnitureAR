Furniture AR

Room scanner setup

Scan with the Furniture AR Scan app, then upload the room file to your layout.

Requires an iPhone or iPad with LiDAR. Install the scanner using Xcode on a Mac. It isn’t available in the App Store.

Install the scanner

  1. On your Mac, open ios/FurnitureARScan/FurnitureARScan.xcodeproj from the Furniture AR project.
  2. In Signing & Capabilities, choose your Apple development team with automatic signing.
  3. Connect and unlock your iPhone. Select it in Xcode and press Run. Follow any prompts to trust the Mac or enable Developer Mode.

The project README includes signing help.

Scan your room

  1. Open Furniture AR Scan, allow camera access, and tap Scan my room.
  2. Move slowly in good light. Include every wall, doorway, and piece of furniture.
  3. Tap Finish scan. Check the outline and give the room a name.
Open scanner

Import the scan

Choose Save / share room file → Save to Files. Return here, choose Room scan → Upload a room scan, and select the JSON file. You can also AirDrop it to your Mac.

Review the outline, then choose Create layout from scan. The app’s Open in Furniture AR button still opens the earlier Eric site; use the saved file to continue here.

Adjust existing objects in the layout to correct any missed or inaccurate scans. Added products show warnings when they overlap furniture or doorways.

Check scan measurements before planning. AR shows added products and needs manual alignment. Camera images stay on your device; layouts stay in this browser.
