Room scanner setup

Scan the walls and furniture already in your room with the companion iPhone app. Import the outlines into a layout, then plan the pieces you want to add around them.

Works on iPhone 16 Pro and other iPhones with LiDAR. The scan uses Apple’s LiDAR room scanner through a companion iPhone app. Install it from Xcode on your Mac first; the website handles importing and arranging your room.

Install the scanner

  1. On your Mac, open ios/FurnitureARScan/FurnitureARScan.xcodeproj from the Furniture AR project.
  2. In Signing & Capabilities, choose your Apple development team with automatic signing.
  3. Connect and unlock your iPhone. Select it in Xcode and press Run. Follow any prompts to trust the Mac or enable Developer Mode.

This is a development app, not an App Store download. The project’s README includes the installation and signing steps.

Scan your room

  1. Open Furniture AR Scan, allow camera access, and tap Scan my room.
  2. Move slowly in good light. Include every wall, doorway, corner, and piece of furniture.
  3. Tap Finish scan. Check the outline and give the room a name.
Open installed scanner

Install the companion app before using this button.

Import the scan

Tap Open in Furniture AR in the scanner. Review the room on the website, then choose Create layout from scan.

You can also use Save / share room file to save to Files or AirDrop to your Mac. Choose Import room scan in the website and select that room file.

Existing objects appear as fixed, shaded outlines. Select them to correct the measurements, remove a mistaken detection, or add anything the scan missed. New products remain movable, with warnings when they overlap obstacles or doorways.

Scans are estimates. Check the outline and important measurements; rescan if walls are missing. AR previews show your new products and require manual alignment. Camera images stay on your phone. Layouts save in each browser and do not sync across devices.
