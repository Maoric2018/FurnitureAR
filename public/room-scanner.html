Room scanner setup

Scan the walls and furniture already in your room with the companion iPhone app. Import the outlines into a layout, then plan the pieces you want to add around them.

Works on iPhone 16 Pro and other iPhones with LiDAR. The scan uses Apple’s LiDAR room scanner through a companion iPhone app. Install it from Xcode on your Mac first; the website handles importing and arranging your room.

Install the scanner

  1. On your Mac, open ios/FurnitureARScan/FurnitureARScan.xcodeproj from the Roomify project.
  2. In Signing & Capabilities, choose your Apple development team with automatic signing.
  3. Connect and unlock your iPhone. Select it in Xcode and press Run. Follow any prompts to trust the Mac or enable Developer Mode.

This is a development app, not an App Store download. The project’s README includes the installation and signing steps.

Scan your room

  1. Open Roomify Scan, allow camera access, and tap Scan my room.
  2. Move slowly in good light. Include every wall, doorway, corner, and piece of furniture.
  3. Tap Finish scan. Check the outline and give the room a name.
Open installed scanner

Install the companion app before using this button.

Import the scan

Tap Open in Roomify in the scanner. The planner opens inside the app. Review the room, then choose Create layout from scan.

You can also use Save / share room file to save to Files or AirDrop to your Mac. Open the exported room link or use the file chooser when reviewing an imported scan.

Existing objects appear as fixed, shaded outlines. Select them to correct the measurements, remove a mistaken detection, or add anything the scan missed. New products remain movable, with warnings when they overlap obstacles or doorways.

Choose 3D & AR → View layout in your room. The app keeps the scan’s position or tries to recognize your saved room. Check the room outline against your walls. If recognition fails, choose Match a wall and tap its two floor corners as shown.

Install the updated iPhone app and use the updated planner website. The scanner’s Planner address setting accepts an HTTPS development preview.

Scans are estimates. Check the outline and important measurements; rescan if walls are missing. Tracking maps stay on your phone. The designer receives room measurements and any photos you choose. Ordinary browser AR requires manual placement. Layouts save separately in the app and browser; they do not sync between devices.
