Your real room.
A better starting point.

Scan the walls and furniture already around you. Bring their outlines into your layout, then plan the pieces you want to add.

Your iPhone 16 Pro is supported.
The scan uses Apple’s LiDAR room scanner through a companion iPhone app. Install it from Xcode on your Mac first; the website handles importing and arranging your room.

01 · Install the scanner

  1. On your Mac, open ios/FurnitureARScan/FurnitureARScan.xcodeproj from the Furniture AR project.
  2. In Signing & Capabilities, choose your Apple development team with automatic signing.
  3. Connect and unlock your iPhone. Select it in Xcode and press Run. Follow any prompts to trust the Mac or enable Developer Mode.
This is a development app, not an App Store download. The project’s README includes the installation and signing steps.

02 · Walk around your room

  1. Open Furniture AR Scan, allow camera access, and tap Scan my room.
  2. Move slowly in good light. Include every wall, doorway, corner, and piece of furniture.
  3. Tap Finish scan. Check the outline and give the room a name.
Open installed scanner ↗Install the companion app before using this button.

03 · Bring it into your layout

Tap Open in Furniture AR in the scanner. The planner opens inside the app. Review your room, then choose Create layout from scan.

Existing objects appear as fixed, shaded outlines. Add Amazon products or use Design my room, then arrange the new pieces.

Choose 3D & AR → View layout in your room. The app keeps the scan’s position or tries to recognize your saved room. Check the room outline against your walls. If recognition fails, choose Match a wall and tap its two floor corners as shown.

Install the updated iPhone app and use the updated planner website. The scanner’s Planner address setting also accepts an HTTPS development preview.

Scans are estimates; confirm important measurements. Tracking maps stay on your phone. The designer receives room measurements and any photos you choose. Ordinary browser AR requires manual placement. Layouts save separately in the app and browser; they do not sync between devices.
